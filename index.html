Why I build websites this way

Most people design a website to look good. I design mine to convert.

The moment someone lands on your page, they're deciding whether to trust you or leave. I come from data and social science, and I've spent years looking at why people actually do that.

Most small business sites waste that moment: cluttered, slow, no clear next step. So before I design anything, I ask what a visitor is actually trying to do on that page, and I build around that answer, not around what looks good in a mockup.

And there's a bigger reason this matters. Your website is the only thing online you actually own. Instagram can lock you out. A failed verification check can wipe years of followers overnight. Your website can't be taken from you, if it's built right.

Why have a website?

Instagram can bring people to your business. A website gives them somewhere to go.

  • You own the space Your website gives your business a dedicated online presence that isn't dependent on a social feed or algorithm.
  • Everything has a place Services, pricing, booking information, FAQs and your business story can all live in one easy-to-find place.
  • It helps build trust A professional website gives potential customers a place to understand who you are before deciding to contact or buy from you.
  • Social media and websites work together Instagram can capture attention. Your website can turn that attention into an enquiry, booking or sale.

Social media is valuable for reaching people, building awareness and keeping customers engaged. But your Instagram page is still a space that exists on someone else's platform.

If your business relies entirely on social media, important information such as your services, prices, contact details and story can become difficult for customers to find. Platforms change, algorithms change, and accounts can sometimes become inaccessible.

A website gives your business its own central home online, somewhere you control, where customers can always learn about you, explore what you offer and take the next step.
